Ride Responsibly

Good journeys start with good preparation.

Every journey needs planning, communication and sensible decisions. Passenger safety and comfort should always come first.

Before departure

  • ✓ Confirm the route, pickup and destination.
  • ✓ Discuss travel duration, breaks and weather.
  • ✓ Discuss luggage limits and passenger arrangements.
  • ✓ Clarify the total cost and what it covers.

During the journey

  • ✓ Use appropriate safety equipment for the selected vehicle.
  • ✓ Follow applicable traffic rules and passenger requirements.
  • ✓ Take breaks and communicate discomfort promptly.
  • ✓ Adjust or postpone travel when conditions are unsafe.

Travel requests are subject to availability, suitable conditions, applicable transport regulations and insurance requirements. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you hit the road.

What travel options can I enquire about?

You can enquire about motorcycle (pillion) journeys, car journeys and other possible travel arrangements. Share your route and preferred option so feasibility can be discussed.

Can I request an intercity or long-distance journey?

You can enquire about your preferred route, including intercity and longer journeys. Each request needs to be reviewed for availability, feasibility, applicable rules and the arrangements required.

Can I choose my pickup point and departure time?

Share your preferred pickup location and approximate time. Flexibility can be discussed, but final arrangements depend on the route, schedule and other practical factors.

How much does a journey cost?

Cost depends on the journey and the agreed arrangements. Confirm the total cost, any applicable charges and what is included before agreeing to travel.

Can I travel with luggage?

Mention your luggage details in the enquiry. Luggage capacity depends on the selected vehicle. Mention the amount and size of your baggage so suitability can be discussed and confirmed before departure.

Does submitting the form confirm my journey?

No. The form opens a WhatsApp message for discussion. Your journey is not confirmed until availability, arrangements, applicable requirements and costs have been agreed upon.
